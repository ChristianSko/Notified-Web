FAQ

Still wondering?

How does Notified save notifications?

Notified adds a shortcut that runs when a notification from one of your chosen apps arrives. It passes a copy to Notified, which keeps it on your iPhone. You set this up once in the Shortcuts app.

Does it save notifications from before I set it up?

No. iPhone doesn't keep a history, so Notified can only save notifications that arrive after the shortcut is on. From then on, everything from your chosen apps is kept.

Which apps are saved?

Only the apps you add to the automation. You can add more at any time in the Shortcuts app, and apps without any saved notifications don't show up in Notified.

Does Notified send my notifications anywhere?

No. Notifications are saved and sorted on your iPhone. Summaries are created on‑device with Apple Intelligence on supported devices, and sign‑in codes never go into a summary. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free keeps your last 24 hours with the Lock Screen‑style list, search and filters. Pro adds your full history beyond 24 hours, on‑device summaries, Insights, accent colors and backgrounds, codes protection and the Face ID lock, and removes the ad. Pro is available monthly, yearly or as a one‑time lifetime purchase.

Which iPhones are supported?

Notified runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Summaries need a device that supports Apple Intelligence; on other devices the Today card shows a plain count of what came in.
