Privacy Policy for Notified

Introduction

Welcome to Notified. Notified keeps a history of the notifications you choose to save, so you can find them later. That history is personal, so we built the app to keep it on your device. This privacy policy explains what information is processed, where it goes, and your rights regarding your data.

Your Notification History Stays on Your Device

Notified saves notifications through a Shortcuts automation that you set up yourself. The automation passes each notification (the app's name, its bundle identifier, the title, the text and the time it arrived) to Notified, which stores it in a database on your iPhone.

  • Your notification history is never sent to us or to any server
  • It is not synced to iCloud by Notified. If your device backup is on, it is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, like other app data
  • Notifications older than your retention setting (90 days by default) are deleted automatically. You can choose 7, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, or keep everything. Notifications you mark as saved are kept until you delete them
  • You can delete any notification in the app at any time

Other settings, such as your category rules, accent color, background photo and preferences, are also stored only on your device.

Apple Intelligence

AI summaries and automatic sorting into categories use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Your notifications are not sent to us, to Apple or to any other AI provider for this.

Notifications sorted as Codes (such as one-time passwords) are never included in summaries or AI prompts.

App Icons

To show each app's icon, Notified looks it up with Apple's public App Store search service, using the app's bundle identifier (for example, com.apple.MobileSMS) and your App Store country. This means Apple receives the bundle identifiers of apps that sent you notifications, along with the network information any web request includes.

The content of your notifications is never part of this request. Each icon is downloaded once and stored on your device.

Face ID and Code Protection

You can lock Notified with Face ID and hide codes until you tap them. Both are optional and off by default. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS: Notified only learns whether authentication succeeded and never has access to your face data.

Information We Collect

Analytics Data

We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service, to understand how people use Notified. We only send a handful of fixed events, such as the app being opened, onboarding being completed or the membership screen being shown.

Important: TelemetryDeck does not collect any personally identifiable information, and we never send notification content, app names, search terms or anything you type. TelemetryDeck uses an anonymized identifier based on your device's vendor identifier, which is specific to our apps and cannot be used to track you across other companies' apps.

Subscription and Purchase Information

Purchases, subscriptions and tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase status. This service processes:

  • Purchase status and subscription details
  • A randomly generated customer identifier
  • The anonymized TelemetryDeck identifier, so we can see whether the membership screen leads to purchases
  • No payment information is stored by our app or by us

Feature Requests

When you use Feature Requests:

  • Your request title, description and any comments you post are collected and may be shown publicly to other Notified users after review
  • Your votes on other feature requests are stored with a random identifier created for the feature board, which is not linked to your identity
  • Your email address is optional and only used, with your consent, to tell you when a request is completed. You can unsubscribe at any time
  • This data is stored on our own feature request service, hosted on Cloudflare in the European Union

Bug Reports and Email Support

When you report a bug or contact support, the message is sent from your own Mail app, so you see everything before you send it. It may include:

  • Your message and, optionally, a screenshot you choose
  • App version and build, iOS version, device type and language, for troubleshooting
  • Your email address, so we can reply

Advertising

The free version shows one banner for another independent app in Settings, provided by Kickstart Exchange. Notified Pro removes it. Kickstart Exchange matches apps, not people: it does not use your advertising identifier, track you, or receive any of your notifications. It receives app-level details only (such as Notified's version and your App Store country), Apple's signed confirmation that this is a genuine App Store install, and whether the banner was shown or tapped. See the Kickstart Exchange privacy notice.

Device Permissions

Notifications

Notified may ask for notification permission so it can send you local notifications, such as a test notification while you set up the Shortcuts automation. These are created on your device. Notified cannot read your other apps' notifications on its own; it only receives the ones your automation sends to it.

Photos

If you pick a photo as a background, only that photo is used, through Apple's photo picker, and it stays on your device.

Third-Party Services

We use carefully selected third-party services:

  • Analytics: TelemetryDeck for privacy-focused usage analytics
  • Purchases: Apple and RevenueCat for subscriptions, lifetime purchases and tips
  • App icons: Apple's App Store search service
  • Advertising (free version only): Kickstart Exchange
  • Feature Requests: our own service, hosted on Cloudflare in the EU

None of these services receives the content of your notifications.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • The content of your notifications
  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone), unless you choose to send it
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Calendar or health data
  • Photos or media files
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Your face data

Children's Privacy

Notified is designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security

  • Your notification history is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS
  • We use industry-standard encryption for any data transmission
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete individual notifications at any time, and choose how long the rest are kept
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Ask us to access or delete feature requests, comments or an email address you gave us
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
